Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

At Voixa English, we want students to feel confident when joining our English-learning program. This Refund Policy explains when refunds may be available for our subscription services.

1. Subscription Refunds

Voixa English offers a monthly subscription for $29.99 per month.

If you are not satisfied with your first subscription purchase, you may request a full refund of your initial subscription payment within 7 days of the original purchase.

To request a refund, contact us at:

support@voixaenglish.com

Please include the email address associated with your Voixa English account so that we can locate your subscription.

2. After the 7-Day Refund Period

After the 7-day refund period has ended, subscription payments are generally non-refundable.

If you cancel your subscription after the 7-day refund period, you will retain access to the Services according to our Cancellation Policy and will not be charged for future billing periods after your cancellation takes effect.

3. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, charged more than once, or experienced another billing error, please contact us at support@voixaenglish.com.

We will review the issue and, when appropriate, issue a refund or correction.

4. Service-Related Issues

If a significant technical problem prevents you from accessing the Services you paid for, please contact us as soon as possible.

We will review the circumstances and may provide an appropriate refund, credit, or other resolution when warranted.

5. Class Cancellations or Changes

If Voixa English cancels a scheduled class and is unable to provide an appropriate replacement or alternative arrangement, we may provide a credit, rescheduled class, or refund depending on the circumstances.

Refunds are not automatically provided for a student's failure to attend a scheduled class.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will generally be returned to the original payment method used for the purchase.

Once a refund has been issued, the time required for the funds to appear in your account may depend on your bank or payment provider.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact:

support@voixaenglish.com

Please provide:

  • Your name
  • The email address associated with your Voixa English account
  • The date of the charge
  • A brief explanation of your refund request

We may request additional information when necessary to verify the transaction.

8. Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe that your payment method was used without your authorization, please contact us immediately.

We may investigate suspected fraudulent transactions and take appropriate action to protect our users and our Services.

9. Changes to This Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Any changes will apply to purchases made after the updated policy becomes effective, unless otherwise required by applicable law.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Refund Policy or would like to request a refund, please contact us:

Email: support@voixaenglish.com
