Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Voixa English provides online English-language learning services, including lessons, small-group classes, and related educational resources.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices available to you when you use the Voixa English website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you create an account or use our services, we may collect information you provide to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your account login information
  • Your English learning level and related student information
  • Information you provide during student onboarding
  • Information you provide when contacting us for support
  • Information related to your enrollment, classes, lessons, and learning progress

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to provide and improve our services.

Payment Information

When you purchase a subscription, payment information is processed by our third-party payment processor, Stripe.

Voixa English does not directly store your full credit card number or complete payment card details on our servers. Stripe processes payment information according to its own privacy policy and security practices.

We may receive and store information related to your payment and subscription, such as your subscription status, payment status, transaction information, and Stripe customer or subscription identifiers, so that we can provide your subscription and manage your account.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages or features you access
  • Date and time of access
  • Technical information necessary to maintain the security and operation of the service

We may use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website and services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide English lessons and educational services
  • Enroll you in classes and manage your participation
  • Provide access to your student dashboard and learning materials
  • Track your learning progress
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Manage subscription status and billing
  • Respond to questions and support requests
  • Send account-related emails, including password-reset and service-related messages
  • Maintain the security of our website and services
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access
  • Improve our website, lessons, and services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to providing, securing, or improving our services unless we have a lawful basis to do so or you have provided appropriate consent.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers when necessary to operate Voixa English and provide our services.

These providers may include:

Stripe

We use Stripe to process subscription payments. Payment information is provided directly to Stripe when you make a purchase.

Stripe may collect and process personal and payment information according to its own privacy practices.

Email and Service Providers

We may use third-party providers to deliver transactional emails, operate our infrastructure, store data, or provide other technical services necessary to operate Voixa English.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or legal process
  • Protect the rights, safety, or property of Voixa English, our users, or others
  • Detect or prevent fraud, security issues, or abuse
  • Enforce our Terms of Service

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Voixa English may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage, and session storage to operate the website and maintain your session.

For example, our application may use browser storage to maintain authentication information and remember certain account or session settings.

We do not use browser storage to store your full payment card information.

Third-party services, such as Stripe, may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with their services.

5. Account and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we maintain from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and should notify us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain your account, fulfill our legal or contractual obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and protect our legitimate business interests.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes, we may delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding the personal information we maintain about you, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Ask questions about how your information is used
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Some information may need to be retained when required by law or when necessary to complete a transaction, maintain financial records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or protect our legal rights.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information below.

8. Children's Privacy

Voixa English is intended for adults and is not directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at support@voixaenglish.com. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

Our website may contain links to or integrations with third-party services.

Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites or services that we do not operate.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.
