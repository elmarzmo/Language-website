Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Voixa English offers a monthly subscription for $29.99 per month. This Cancellation Policy explains how you can cancel your subscription and what happens after cancellation.

1. Canceling Your Subscription

You may cancel your Voixa English subscription at any time.

To cancel your subscription, you may use the cancellation option available in your account, or contact us at:

support@voixaenglish.com

Please provide the email address associated with your Voixa English account when contacting us about cancellation.

2. When Cancellation Takes Effect

When you cancel your subscription, your cancellation will generally take effect at the end of your current billing period.

You will continue to have access to the subscription Services until the end of the period you have already paid for, unless your account is suspended or terminated for another reason.

After the current billing period ends, your subscription will not automatically renew and you will not be charged for another billing period.

3. Cancellation and Refunds

Canceling a subscription does not automatically result in a refund for the current billing period.

Refund requests are handled according to our Refund Policy.

If you are within the 7-day refund period for your initial subscription payment, you may be eligible for a full refund as described in our Refund Policy.

4. Scheduled Classes After Cancellation

If you cancel your subscription while you have upcoming classes scheduled during your current billing period, you may continue to attend those classes until your subscription access ends, subject to the applicable class schedule.

Cancellation does not guarantee that a cancelled or missed class will be rescheduled or refunded.

5. Failed Payments

If a subscription payment fails, Voixa English may temporarily restrict access to subscription Services.

We may attempt to collect the outstanding payment through our payment processor.

If the payment remains unsuccessful, the subscription may be cancelled or terminated.

6. Reactivating Your Subscription

If your subscription has been cancelled, you may be able to start a new subscription in the future, subject to the plans and pricing available at that time.

A new subscription may be subject to the terms and pricing displayed when you subscribe.

7. Cancellation by Voixa English

Voixa English may suspend or terminate a subscription if:

  • You violate our Terms of Service
  • We detect fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Your use of the Services creates a security or safety risk
  • We are required to do so by law
  • We discontinue the Services

If we terminate a subscription for reasons other than a violation of our Terms of Service, fraud, or other misconduct, we may provide an appropriate refund or credit for unused Services when warranted.

8. How to Contact Us

If you have questions about cancellation or need assistance cancelling your subscription, contact:

support@voixaenglish.com
